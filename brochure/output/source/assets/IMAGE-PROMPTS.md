# Scaffold image provenance

These four original scaffold photographs were generated with the built-in
OpenAI image-generation tool in `photorealistic-natural` mode. They should be
replaced by commissioned Bayes Institute photography before publication.

## `cover-collaboration.png`

Premium candid editorial photograph of four university-age learners intensely
collaborating around notebooks, a laptop, sketches, and a physical prototype in
a contemporary studio; warm daylight, burgundy and muted teal accents, layered
depth, no logos, readable text, handshakes, or stock-photo posing.

## `mentor-seminar.png`

Tall documentary photograph of an Indian faculty mentor presenting a difficult
idea to a small learner circle in a diagram-covered seminar studio; focused
expressions, natural gestures, directional daylight, and crop-friendly
architectural lines; no podium, brands, or staged classroom behavior.

## `prototype-work.png`

Wide table-level photograph of three learners measuring, sketching, and
adjusting a compact physical prototype in a maker studio; tactile wood and paper
materials, warm side light, accurate hands and tools, and no lab-coat or CGI
aesthetic.

## `field-learning.png`

Vertical humanist documentary photograph of two Indian learners walking through
a contemporary public-space field site while discussing observations and
carrying notebooks; late-afternoon light, grounded confidence, and architectural
negative space; no tourist pose, brands, or camera-facing expressions.
