# Bayes Institute: Website Structure & Landing Page Copy

This document outlines the high-level structure and core content for the Bayes Institute landing page and website, based on the vision and business strategies.

## 1. Homepage (`/`)

The homepage serves to introduce the Institute, explain the "wound" we address (Entrepreneurial Paralysis), and guide users to our core offerings.

*   **Hero Section:**
    *   **Headline:** BECOME MORE.
    *   **Subheadline:** Bayes Institute exists to create the conditions in which human possibility becomes human capability. We are the bridge between wanting to build something meaningful and being ready to bet your life on building it.
    *   **Primary Action:** "Explore the Fellowship" (routes to `/fellowship`)
*   **The Problem (The Wound):**
    *   Address *Entrepreneurial Paralysis*. The gap between technical capability and commercial courage.
*   **Flagship Offering (The Bayes Founder Fellowship):**
    *   A 40-week hybrid fellowship for builders.
    *   **Hook:** "FIND OUT." (Am I actually capable of building something meaningful? Find out.)
*   **Our Core Principles:**
    *   1. See Possibility Before Proof
    *   2. Put Ideas Under Load
    *   3. Keep the Bar High, Build More Ladders
    *   4. Own What Happens Next
    *   5. Let Truth Revise Us
*   **Ecosystem Teasers:**
    *   Brief links/sections previewing the **Ideas** repository and the **Library**.

## 2. Fellowship Page (`/fellowship`)

A dedicated page explaining the Bayes Founder Fellowship.

*   **Overview:** A 40-week part-time hybrid fellowship for technical builders, engineers, and domain experts.
*   **The Curriculum Architecture:**
    *   *Build:* Weekly missions to make learning visible.
    *   *Observe:* Industry expeditions and unfiltered Founder Files.
    *   *Question:* The Founder Hot Seat and Pitch Perfect.
*   **Pricing & Scholarship:** Transparent explanation of the tuition, zero-debt policy, and the No-Quit Guarantee.
*   **Primary CTA:** "Claim Your Founder Seat" / "Apply Now" (routes to `/admissions`)

## 3. Admissions Portal (`/admissions`)

The application process is split into three distinct, high-conviction sections:

*   **Section A (Technical Trajectory & Signal Audit):**
    *   Evaluates obsession, agency, and ability to execute. Asks candidates to describe a system they built or a technical rabbit hole they fell into.
*   **Section B (Ambition & Origin Essays):**
    *   Reflections on formative building experiences, childhood ambitions, and personal drive.
*   **Section C (Fellowship & Scholarship Aid Request):**
    *   Financial assistance bracket selection.
    *   Merit prompt: "Why will your presence elevate the caliber and execution energy of this cohort?"

*After applying, candidates are directed to a 1-on-1 Founder Diagnostic Interview.*

## 4. Ideas Page (`/ideas`)

The engine for acquisition and our unique USP.

*   **Headline:** 1,500+ Raw Ideas Awaiting Execution.
*   **Description:** We are India's only institute with thousands of raw, vetted ideas sitting to be worked upon. These represent unaddressed market frictions, waiting for the right founder.
*   **Features:**
    *   Searchable/filterable directory of 1,500 - 2,000 ideas.
    *   Categories: B2B, B2C, Healthcare, SaaS, Logistics, etc.
*   **Acquisition CTA:** Find an idea that obsesses you? Reach out and start working on it with Bayes.

## 5. Library Page (`/library`)

An open repository of institutional knowledge.

*   **Content:** Tech essays, architecture critiques, teardowns, and B2B sales playbooks.
*   **Purpose:** To build trust before a candidate applies, proving the rigor of our Pedagogical Operating System.

## 6. Companies Page (`/companies`) - *Future Expansion*

*   **Content:** A portfolio showcase of the companies incorporated within the institute, tracking their revenue milestones, founders, and the 10x wedges they are building.
